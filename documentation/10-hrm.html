Module 10 — Human Resource Management (HRM)

Module: Human Resource Management (HRM)
Category: Workforce
Purpose: Manages employees, payroll disbursements, and task assignment — with task-completion analytics feeding the Business Intelligence dashboard.

HRM keeps the workforce ledger: employee master data, payroll runs per employee, and a task board. Payroll payments flow into the finance module as salaryPayment transactions, and task completion rates drive employee performance analytics.

1. Overview

The HRM module covers three entities:

  • Employees — master records with job title, salary, hire date, and status.
  • Payroll — per-employee payment runs (basic salary + allowances − deductions = net).
  • Tasks — assignments with status and dates, linked to employees.

3. Key Models & Relationships

  • Employee (app/Models/Employee.php) — payrolls() and tasks() has-many; computed full_name accessor; salary decimal, hire_date date.
  • Payroll — employee() belongs-to; net salary derived from basic + allowances − deductions.
  • TaskManagement — employee() belongs-to.

  • Task CRUD tracks assignment, start/due dates, and status lifecycle.

5. Financial Link (Payroll)

Payroll payments should be recorded as salaryPayment transactions with corresponding cashbook outflow entries — following the transactional pattern established by the Sales/Finance modules. Net salary = basic_salary + allowances − deductions.

6. Analytics & BI

  • Dashboard widget — top employees by task completion rate (legacy feature list).
  • Flask BI employee analysis — POST /api/analyze clusters employees with KMeans (n=3) and flags anomalies with IsolationForest. Composite performance score = 0.4 × completion_rate + 0.3 × (1 − avg_task_hours) + 0.3 × completed_tasks (scaled 0–100). View: GET /bi/employees.
