Module 09 — Manufacturing

Module: Manufacturing (Manufacturing)
Category: Production
Purpose: Plans and executes in-house production through three linked records — Bill of Materials (BOM), Production Planning, and Productions — converting raw inputs into finished goods.

Manufacturing gives the ERP a production view: a bill of materials defines how a product is built, a production plan estimates cost and profit for a quantity, and a production run captures the executed batch.

1. Overview

The module follows a three-stage model:

  1. Bill of Materials — the recipe: which product, component quantities, and unit.
  2. Production Planning — the estimate: production cost, other cost, expected profit, quantity, and a finalize flag.
  3. Production — the execution: actual batch with cost/profit and quantity.

3. Key Models & Relationships

  • BillOfMaterial — product() belongs-to (the finished good); productionPlannings() has-many; productions() has-many.
  • ProductionPlanning — billOfMaterial() belongs-to; finalized enum gates whether the plan is ready for execution.
  • Production — billOfMaterial() belongs-to; stores the executed batch economics.

5. Financial Link

Production costs flow into the ledger as bomExpense transactions (see Finance module). When a production run is created or a plan finalized, cost figures (production cost + other cost) should be reflected in the transaction ledger and cashbook — follow the transactional pattern for any money-moving step.
