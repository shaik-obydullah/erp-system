Module 08 — Procurement & Supply Chain

Module: Procurement & Supply Chain (Procurement)
Category: Inbound Operations
Purpose: Manages the purchase cycle from need identification → purchase order → shipment → receipt → returns, with supplier due tracking and a supplier-facing portal.

Procurement converts identified needs into supplier purchase orders, then tracks physical fulfillment through warehouses. Every purchase order carries a due amount that feeds the finance module's payable ledger.

1. Overview

The procurement pipeline:

  1. Need — a recorded requirement (description + date identified).
  2. Purchase Order — formal order to a supplier for a need, with total amount and due amount.
  3. Shipment — physical delivery tracked against the purchase order, routed to a warehouse.
  4. Shipment Return — defective/excess items returned to the supplier.

3. Key Models & Relationships

  • Need — purchaseOrders() has-many.
  • PurchaseOrder — need() and supplier() belongs-to; shipments(), shipmentReturns() has-many.
  • Shipment — purchaseOrder() and warehouse() belongs-to.
  • ShipmentReturn — purchaseOrder() belongs-to.
  • Supplier — Authenticatable with portal access; balance tracks payables (see Finance module).

Key flows:

  • PurchaseOrderController::store — validates need, supplier, unique order_number, total ≥ 0, due ≥ 0; writes order and logs activity.
  • ShipmentController — creates a shipment against a PO + warehouse with a unique tracking number and status lifecycle.
  • ShipmentReturnController — records return of goods (invoice amount, reason, status).

5. Supplier Portal

SupplierPortalController (auth:supplier + active.supplier, prefix supplier-portal):

  • GET /supplier-portal — dashboard.
  • GET /supplier-portal/purchase-orders — the supplier's purchase orders (POs link by fk_supplier_id).
  • GET /supplier-portal/purchase-orders/{id} — PO detail.
  • GET /supplier-portal/products — products supplied by this supplier.
  • GET/PUT /supplier-portal/profile — profile view/update.

Supplier authentication is covered in the Auth module (routes in routes/supplier.php).

6. Supplier Transactions & Due Management

  • SupplierTransactionController — GET /suppliers/transactions with export/import (permission:suppliers.view).
  • SupplierFundController — fund deposits / payments to suppliers: updates supplier balance, writes supplierDeposit / supplierPayment transactions and cashbook rows transactionally.
  • SupplierDueController — GET /suppliers/due payable overview.
  • Supplier balances appear in reports.suppliers (positive/negative totals).

8. Financial Link

Purchase orders contribute to supplier payables (due amounts). Payments reduce the payable via supplier-fund workflows; each is recorded in the transactions and cashbook ledgers. Any new payment flow must follow the transactional pattern.
