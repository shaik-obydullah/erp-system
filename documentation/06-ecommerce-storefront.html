Module 06 — E-Commerce & Storefront

Module: E-Commerce & Storefront (Ecommerce)
Category: Multi-Vendor Sales Channel
Purpose: Runs the public multi-vendor storefront, shopping cart, checkout, customer portal, supplier portal, and social features (reviews, AI search, AI support chat).

This module turns the ERP into a marketplace. Customers browse vendor stores, add to cart, checkout with billing/shipping, track orders, and leave reviews — while suppliers get a portal to view their products and purchase orders.

1. Overview

The e-commerce module spans three audiences:

  • Shoppers (customers) — storefront pages, cart, checkout, customer portal, product reviews, AI search and support chat.
  • Suppliers — supplier portal with their products and purchase orders.
  • Admins — CMS content, review moderation, flash sales, campaign content.

4. Customer Authentication & Portal

Routes in routes/customer.php (prefix customer) and routes/web.php (prefix portal):

  • Auth: register, login, forgot/reset password, logout — all behind guest:customer / auth:customer + active.customer.
  • Portal (CustomerPortalController): dashboard, order history, order detail, profile view/update.
  • Customer-facing auth controllers under app/Http/Controllers/Customer/Auth/.

5. Supplier Portal

Routes in routes/supplier.php (prefix supplier) and routes/web.php (prefix supplier-portal):

  • Auth: login, forgot/reset password — behind guest:supplier / auth:supplier + active.supplier.
  • Portal (SupplierPortalController): dashboard, purchase orders list + detail, their products, profile view/update.
  • Auth controllers under app/Http/Controllers/Supplier/Auth/.

6. Reviews

reviews ties a product to a user with a 1–5 rating and optional text. Products denormalize review_number and review_avg for list displays. Reviews flow: customer submits → admin moderates (published/unpublished).

7. AI Storefront Integration

Public AI endpoints served by AiController (no token required):

EndpointPurpose
GET /api/v1/ai/statusHealth check — returns { available, model } so the UI can hide/disable AI widgets when Ollama is offline
POST /api/v1/ai/customer-supportSupport chat; detects product keywords in the message, enriches context with up to 5 matching products (name, sku, price, availability), and returns an Ollama answer

Protected AI endpoints (POS/admin): product-description, product-search, inventory-insights, sales-forecast, price-suggestion.

8. Flash Sales & CMS

  • Flash sales — time-boxed percentage discounts on specific stocks; surfaced on the storefront as promotional blocks ("flash sale products", "new arrivals", "best sellers").
  • CMS content — contents records render as pages/banners; type and sort_order drive placement on the homepage.

9. Checkout & Order Fulfillment

  1. Customer completes billing + shipping forms; order summary shows subtotal, shipping, tax, VAT, total.
  2. Sale of type Ecommerce is created with status orderPlaced; billing and shipping rows are stored.
  3. Order progresses: orderPlaced → processing → shipment → completed (or canceled).
  4. Customer tracks status in the portal.
