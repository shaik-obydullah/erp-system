Module 12 — AI & Business Intelligence

Module: AI & Business Intelligence (AI / BI)
Category: Intelligence Layer
Purpose: Brings self-hosted AI (Ollama / llama3.2) and a Python BI microservice (Flask + scikit-learn) to the ERP — generating product copy, natural-language search, insights, forecasts, pricing advice, support chat, and ML-driven analytics.

All AI runs self-hosted at zero ongoing API cost with full data privacy. Ollama provides generative features through App\Services\OllamaService; a Flask microservice provides ML analytics (KMeans clustering, IsolationForest anomaly detection, item-based recommendations, and forecasting). Both are optional — every caller must handle an unavailable service gracefully.

1. Overview

Two intelligence engines power the system:

  • Ollama (llama3.2) — generative AI for product descriptions, product search, inventory insights, sales forecasting, price suggestions, and customer support. Config: config/services.php, env OLLAMA_URL (default http://erp_ollama:11434), AI_MODEL (default llama3.2).
  • Flask BI microservice — ML analytics at http://bi_flask:5000/api, consumed by BiController. Endpoints: /analyze, /product-analysis, /sales-forecast, /recommendations/products, /product-combos, /prophet-forecast.

4. Business Intelligence Microservice (Flask)

The Flask app (referenced as bi_flask:5000 in Docker) exposes ML endpoints consumed by BiController:

/api/analyzePOSTStandardScaler + KMeans (n=3) + IsolationForest (contamination 0.05); composite score 0.4·completion_rate + 0.3·(1−avg_task_hours) + 0.3·completed_tasksEmployee performance groups (High/Average/Low), anomalies

Models are persisted with joblib (scaler, KMeans, IsolationForest, KNN, collaborative filter). There is a model-training endpoint: POST /api/recommendations/train.

6. Availability & Graceful Degradation

  • Ollama — always check isAvailable() / GET /api/v1/ai/status before enabling AI widgets; treat null responses as failures.
  • Flask BI — 30s HTTP timeouts; errors are surfaced as JSON and rendered as empty/error states.
  • Business decisions must never hard-block on AI output — AI augments, it does not gate.

7. Permissions

BI routes require an authenticated active admin (auth:admin + active.admin). No granular permission is attached to /bi/* in the route file.
