Module 11 — Marketing & Campaigns

Module: Marketing & Campaigns (Marketing)
Category: Growth
Purpose: Plans and budgets promotional campaigns, tracks campaign spend through the financial ledger, and exposes time-limited flash-sale pricing on the storefront.

Marketing turns analytics into action. Campaigns carry a budget and date window; campaign expenses are recorded as campaignExpense transactions. Flash sales apply time-boxed percentage discounts directly to stock records for the storefront.

1. Overview

The marketing module includes:

  • Campaigns — named promotions with description, start/end dates, budget, and a status lifecycle (planned/active/completed/canceled).
  • Flash sales — time-limited discounts on specific stock records, surfaced on the storefront.
  • Campaign expense tracking — spend recorded in the finance ledger.

2. Database Tables

TablePurposeKey Columns
campaignsPromotional campaignsname, description, start_date, end_date, status (planned/active/completed/canceled), budget
flash_salesTime-limited discountsname, start_date, end_date, fk_stock_id, discount (percentage)

3. Key Models

  • Campaign — full CRUD; budget is a decimal; status enum drives lifecycle.
  • FlashSale — links a stock to a percentage discount within a datetime window; used by the storefront to render promotional blocks (flash sale / new arrivals / best sellers).

5. Financial Link

Campaign budget spend should be recorded as campaignExpense transactions with cashbook outflow entries, per the transactional pattern. Campaign budget lookups support the UI when creating expenses.
